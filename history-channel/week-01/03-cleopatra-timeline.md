# Day 3: Cleopatra and the Moon Landing

[← Back to channel plan](../README.md) · [Style guide](../style-guide.md)

## 1. Full narration script

Paste this into Google AI Studio → Generate speech (with the voice style line from the style guide), or record it yourself.

```
Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing… than to the building of the Great Pyramid. The Great Pyramid was finished around 2560 BC. Cleopatra was born in 69 BC — about two and a half thousand years later. The Moon landing? 1969 — only about two thousand years after her. To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient — older to her than Jesus is to us. And she wasn't Egyptian by family. She was Greek — from a dynasty founded by one of Alexander the Great's generals. But she was said to be the first of her family to learn the Egyptian language. Ancient Egypt lasted so long, its own history was ancient history. Follow for more stories history forgot.
```
