# Day 1: The King Who Lived on a Rock (Sigiriya)

[← Back to channel plan](../README.md) · [Style guide](../style-guide.md)

## 1. Full narration script

Paste this into Google AI Studio → Generate speech (with the voice style line from the style guide), or record it yourself.

```
Imagine a king so afraid… he built his palace on top of a giant rock. Fifth-century Sri Lanka. Prince Kashyapa seized the throne from his own father. His brother, Moggallana, fled to India, swearing revenge. So Kashyapa built a fortress nearly 200 metres up, on Sigiriya. Visitors climbed up through the mouth of a gigantic stone lion. Today, only its paws remain. Halfway up, painted women still smile from the rock walls… …and a wall polished like a mirror shines beside the path. Centuries later, travellers scratched poems onto it. Around 495 AD, Moggallana returned with an army. Kashyapa rode out on his war elephant — but it turned aside to avoid a swamp. His soldiers thought he was retreating… and fled. After his death, the sky palace was given to Buddhist monks. Follow for more stories history forgot.
```
