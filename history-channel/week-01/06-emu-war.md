# Day 6: The War Australia Lost to Birds

[← Back to channel plan](../README.md) · [Style guide](../style-guide.md)

## 1. Full narration script

Paste this into Google AI Studio → Generate speech (with the voice style line from the style guide), or record it yourself.

```
In 1932, Australia went to war… against birds. And lost. After World War One, many veterans became wheat farmers in Western Australia. Then around twenty thousand emus arrived — eating and trampling their crops. So the government sent soldiers. With machine guns. Major Meredith's team set up ambushes… but the emus split into small groups and scattered. Emus can run up to fifty kilometres an hour. One gun jammed at the worst moment. They mounted a gun on a truck — but the truck couldn't keep up on the rough ground. After about a month, the army pulled out. The newspapers loved it. The army claimed nearly a thousand birds — but the emus kept coming. Follow for more stories history forgot.
```
