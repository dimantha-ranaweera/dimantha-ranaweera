# Day 4: How the Library of Alexandria Really Died

[← Back to channel plan](../README.md) · [Style guide](../style-guide.md)

## 1. Full narration script

Paste this into Google AI Studio → Generate speech (with the voice style line from the style guide), or record it yourself.

```
Everyone says the Library of Alexandria burned in one great fire. That's not what happened. Founded around 300 BC, it had one goal: collect every book in the world. Ships arriving in Alexandria were searched. Any scrolls on board were copied — and the library often kept the originals. In 48 BC, Julius Caesar's fire in the harbour may have burned some books — but the library survived. What really killed it was slower. Kings stopped funding it. Scholars were driven out of the city. And wars struck Alexandria again and again, over hundreds of years. The greatest library in history didn't die in a blaze. It died of neglect. Follow for more stories history forgot.
```
