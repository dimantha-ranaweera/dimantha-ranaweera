# Day 2: Who Really Built the Pyramids?

## 1. Full narration script

Paste this into Google AI Studio → Generate speech (with the voice style line from the style guide), or record it yourself.

```
Everyone thinks slaves built the pyramids. Archaeologists found something else. In the 1990s, right next to the Giza pyramids, they uncovered a whole town built for the workers. Bakeries. Breweries. Long sleeping halls. Workers ate bread, drank beer — and even got meat. Archaeologists found huge numbers of cattle bones. And the biggest clue: tombs. The workers were buried right beside the pyramids, with bread and beer for the afterlife. Some skeletons even show broken bones that had healed — someone gave them medical care. Inside the Great Pyramid, work crews painted their team names — like 'Friends of Khufu'. The pyramids weren't built by whips… but by teams proud to sign their work. Follow for more stories history forgot.
```
