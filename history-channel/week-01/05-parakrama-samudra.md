# Day 5: The King Who Wouldn't Waste a Drop (Parakrama Samudra)

## 1. Full narration script

Paste this into Google AI Studio → Generate speech (with the voice style line from the style guide), or record it yourself.

```
A Sri Lankan king once made a promise: not a single drop of rain would flow into the sea without serving the people. Twelfth century. King Parakramabahu the Great ruled from Polonnaruwa. And he meant it. He built and restored reservoirs and canals all across the island. His greatest work: the Parakrama Samudra — the 'Sea of Parakrama' — made by joining older reservoirs into one. It's so big, many people mistake it for a natural lake. Its embankment runs for around 14 kilometres. And almost nine hundred years later, it still waters rice fields today. Ancient Sri Lanka was a land of master water engineers. So next time you see a wewa, remember — someone planned it almost a thousand years ago. Follow for more stories history forgot.
```
