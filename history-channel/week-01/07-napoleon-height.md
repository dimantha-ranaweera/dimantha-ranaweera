# Day 7: Napoleon Wasn't Short

[← Back to channel plan](../README.md) · [Style guide](../style-guide.md)

## 1. Full narration script

Paste this into Google AI Studio → Generate speech (with the voice style line from the style guide), or record it yourself.

```
Napoleon wasn't short. He was around 1.69 metres — five foot seven. Average, or even a little taller than most Frenchmen of his time. So where did the myth come from? First: French inches were longer than British inches. His height was written as five foot two in French units — and the British read it as five foot two in theirs. Second: British cartoonists drew him as a tiny, angry man throwing tantrums. Third: he was often surrounded by his Imperial Guard — elite soldiers chosen partly for their height. Even his nickname, 'the little corporal', was about affection — not size. Two hundred years later, the cartoon still wins. Follow for more stories history forgot.
```
